Sew What's Next began with a familiar creative problem: a closet full of possibility and no easy way to decide what to make.

## Start with the real decision
The product is not primarily about cataloging fabric or patterns. It is about helping a maker move from *I could make anything* to *this is what I want to make next*.

That framing shaped every feature—from matching patterns and fabric to using OCR to reduce manual entry and AI to make inspiration feel specific.
